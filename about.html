About this app

Quranic Arabic is a personal learning web app: reading and writing the Arabic script, the 150 most frequent Quranic words, and verse study with word-by-word audio.

New here? Read how to learn — the path in order, with the why.

No tracking, no analytics. Your progress is stored on this device — the optional Google sign-in on the Progress page only ever syncs it to your own private account (and you can skip it).
